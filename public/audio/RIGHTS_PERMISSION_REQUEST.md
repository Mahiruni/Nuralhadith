# Nur al-Hadith — Audio Rights Permission Request

**Project:** Nur al-Hadith  
**Project owner:** Mahir Aman / Biloo  
**Repository:** Mahiruni/Nuralhadith

## Purpose

Nur al-Hadith is a multilingual digital library intended to help people read, study, and listen to authenticated collections of Prophetic Hadith ﷺ. We are building the audio system so that every recording is used only under documented permission or an explicit license.

## Rights requested

For recordings for which you control the rights, we respectfully request written permission for:

- Streaming through the Nur al-Hadith website and Progressive Web App (PWA).
- Playback through Android/mobile applications associated with Nur al-Hadith.
- Temporary technical caching required for reliable streaming.
- User-initiated offline download **only if you expressly permit redistribution/offline use**.
- Redistribution of the authorized audio files only where expressly permitted.
- Preservation of the recording's original attribution and rights notices.
- Use in a free educational/da'wah service.
- Commercial use only if separately and expressly authorized; otherwise Nur al-Hadith will treat the permission as non-commercial.

## Requested permission statement

A suitable confirmation could read:

> We confirm that [rights holder] grants Nur al-Hadith permission to stream the specified Hadith audio recordings through its website/PWA and mobile applications. [Offline download and redistribution are permitted / streaming only]. The required attribution is: [attribution]. This permission applies to the recordings listed below and remains subject to the following conditions: [conditions].

A rights holder may use its own wording instead.

## Attribution

Nur al-Hadith will display the attribution supplied by the rights holder and retain the original license/rights information in its public source register.

## Verification standard

Nur al-Hadith will not label an audio recording as **verified-redistribution** until we have either:

1. An explicit published license that grants the required rights; or
2. Written permission from the rights holder or an authorized representative.

Public accessibility alone will not be treated as proof of redistribution rights.

## Languages

We are initially interested in:

- Arabic
- English
- Amharic
- Tigrinya
- Afaan Oromo

Translation recordings will be treated as separate rights assets unless the permission explicitly covers them.

## Evidence retained

For every approved recording, Nur al-Hadith will retain:

- Rights holder
- Permission/license URL or document reference
- Original source URL
- Exact permitted uses
- Attribution requirements
- Verification date
- Recording identifier
- Collection and Hadith number
- Language
- Reciter
- Optional SHA-256 checksum

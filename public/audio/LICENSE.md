# Nur al-Hadith Audio Rights Policy

Audio recordings are **not considered redistributable merely because they are publicly accessible**.

A recording may be bundled, cached for offline use, mirrored, or redistributed by Nur al-Hadith only when its rights record explicitly permits that use.

Each recording must have a corresponding rights manifest containing:

- collection
- Hadith number
- language
- recording URL
- reciter
- rights holder
- license or written permission
- license URL or permission reference
- permitted uses
- attribution text
- verification date
- checksum when bundled

## Required license states

- `verified-redistribution`: redistribution/offline bundling is explicitly permitted.
- `verified-streaming`: streaming/embed is permitted, but offline redistribution is not.
- `permission-required`: permission has not yet been obtained.
- `unknown`: rights could not be established.

Only `verified-redistribution` recordings may be offered through Nur al-Hadith's offline download system.

Nur al-Hadith does not convert, synthesize, remix, or alter a recording and present it as an original prophetic recitation.

Third-party rights remain with the relevant reciter/rightsholder. Attribution must be displayed according to the rights record.

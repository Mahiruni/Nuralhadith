# Nur al-Hadith Audio Source Register

This register is intentionally conservative.

## Sources investigated

### MuslimDawah Hadith Audio
Publicly accessible Hadith MP3 directories exist for several major collections. Public availability alone does not establish redistribution rights, so these files remain **permission-required** until the rights holder gives explicit permission.

### IslamHouse
IslamHouse publishes substantial Islamic audio content, but its published disclaimer for some audio content states that files may be downloaded for personal use only and restricts commercial use; it also says RSS feeds require written permission. Therefore those recordings are **not automatically eligible** for bundling in Nur al-Hadith.

### HadeethEnc / IslamHouse textual content
HadeethEnc/IslamHouse has an explicit reuse policy for its textual content: preservation of original content/metadata and clear attribution are required. This policy should not be interpreted as a blanket audio license.

## Acceptance rule

No recording is marked `verified-redistribution` until a specific recording/rightsholder has an explicit license or written permission covering the intended Nur al-Hadith use.

The app may stream a third-party source only when its terms explicitly permit that form of use. Offline caching/bundling requires the stronger redistribution permission.
